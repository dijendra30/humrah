FOUNDER'S JOURNEY
CHAPTER 01: WHERE IT STARTED

It started in Andaman.

ANDAMAN

Growing up in the quiet surroundings of Andaman, my journey into technology and building things began far away from the bustling tech hubs.

CHAPTER 02: THE MOVE

Then I came to Delhi.

Moving from Andaman to a huge city like Delhi changed the way I saw human connection. A new city. Thousands of people. A completely different environment. Yet, it was still difficult to find genuine companionship.

A CITY FULL OF PEOPLE
CAN STILL FEEL LONELY.

You can be surrounded by people and still have nobody to call when you want to talk, walk, explore the city, grab coffee, or simply spend some time together.

WHAT IF TECHNOLOGY
COULD HELP PEOPLE
FIND REAL CONNECTION?

That's where Humrah began.

I wanted to create something focused on companionship and real-world experiences rather than endless social feeds.

HUMRAH IS NOT A DATING APP.
Real people. Real moments.
