1. Introduction & Data Fiduciary Statement

Humrah operates a real-world companion booking platform that enables users to arrange in-person meetups at public locations. This Privacy Policy describes how Humrah collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal data, and explains the rights available to users.

Data Fiduciary: Humrah is a Data Fiduciary as defined under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). This means Humrah determines the purpose and means of processing your personal data and is responsible for ensuring that processing is lawful, fair, and transparent.

By registering an account or using the Humrah platform, you provide your informed and explicit consent to the collection and processing of your personal data as described in this policy. You may withdraw consent at any time as described in Section 11. Withdrawal of consent may limit or terminate your ability to use the platform.

This policy applies to all users accessing Humrah via the mobile application (Android or iOS) or any related service. It is governed primarily by the DPDP Act, 2023 and applicable rules thereunder. Users are encouraged to read this policy in full before using the platform.

5. Face Verification & Biometric Data

Notice: Humrah collects and processes biometric personal data in the form of face embeddings. This section explains what is collected, how it is used, how long it is retained, and your rights in relation to this data. You will be asked for explicit consent within the application before any biometric processing begins.

What Is Collected

  • Verification Video: A short video recording in which the user performs a liveness detection action. This video is used solely to generate the face embedding and is deleted within 48 hours of processing.
  • Face Embedding: A mathematical numerical representation derived from the verification video. This is classified as biometric personal data and is treated accordingly.

What Face Embeddings Are — and Are Not

Face embeddings are arrays of numbers produced by a machine-learning model applied to facial geometry. They are not photographs or images. They cannot be converted back into a recognizable image of the user. They are not used to identify users outside the Humrah platform and cannot be used for facial recognition in any external system. Their sole function is to compute a similarity score between two embeddings for the purpose of detecting whether the same person has previously created an account on Humrah.

How Face Verification Works

  1. The user records a short liveness verification video within the app after providing explicit consent.
  2. The system processes the video to confirm the presence of a real, live person (liveness check).
  3. A unique face embedding is generated from the video.
  4. The embedding is compared against Humrah's internal database to detect whether an account associated with the same face already exists.
  5. The original verification video is permanently deleted within 48 hours of processing.
  6. The face embedding is retained in encrypted form for the duration of the account and for 30 days following account deletion.

Purpose Limitation

Face embeddings are used solely for the following purposes:

  • Detecting and preventing duplicate or fraudulent account creation.
  • Preventing previously banned users from re-registering on the platform.
  • Confirming that the account holder is a real person (liveness detection).

Face embeddings are not used for age verification, identity authentication beyond the purposes above, marketing, research, or any purpose outside those listed here.

Automated Decision-Making

The comparison of face embeddings against the database is performed through an automated process. This process may result in an automated decision to flag or reject an account registration if a duplicate is detected. If your account registration is rejected or suspended as a result of this automated process, you have the right to request human review of that decision.

Human Review and Appeal

If you believe that an automated face verification decision has been made in error — for example, if your account has been flagged or rejected incorrectly — you may submit an appeal by contacting the Grievance Officer at the details listed in Section 16. Humrah will conduct a manual review of the flagged decision and respond within 15 business days. During this period, the account will remain suspended pending the outcome of the review.

Explicit Consent for Biometric Processing

Before any biometric data is collected, users are presented with a dedicated consent screen within the application that explains: (a) what biometric data will be collected; (b) the specific purpose for which it will be used; (c) the retention period; and (d) the right to withdraw consent and the consequences of doing so. Consent is recorded with a timestamp. If you do not provide this consent, account activation will not be possible, as face verification is a mandatory security requirement of the platform.

Retention Minimization

  • Verification videos are deleted within 48 hours of processing.
  • Face embeddings are retained for the duration of the active account and for 30 days following account deletion, after which they are permanently deleted.
  • No biometric data is retained beyond the periods described above.

Note: Face verification is a mandatory feature of Humrah. If you do not consent to biometric processing, the platform cannot be used. Biometric data is not shared with third parties except as required by law.

Changing Your Profile Photo and Photo Verification

This part explains what happens to your photo verification when you change the profile photo shown on your Humrah profile.

  • Uploading and changing your photo: You can upload a profile photo and change it later in the Humrah app. Humrah processes your profile photo to display your profile and to provide account and profile features. Profile photos are stored with the image storage provider listed in Section 8 and retained as described in Section 9.
  • How photo verification works: Photo verification uses Humrah's existing verification process, which may involve submitting a short video or other material through the app. Authorized Humrah reviewers may review the material you submit.
  • A verification applies to the photo that was reviewed: If you change your displayed profile photo, your previous photo verification no longer applies and your verification status returns to "not submitted". A verification request that is still under review for your previous photo is cancelled and is not used to verify the new photo.
  • Verifying your new photo: You may need to submit your new photo for verification again through the same in-app process. The Humrah app asks you to confirm before you replace a verified profile photo.
  • Features that require verification: Quick Connect and other features that require photo verification may remain unavailable until your new photo is approved.
  • Your previous photo: After your new profile photo has been saved, Humrah requests deletion of your previous profile photo from its image storage. If saving the new photo fails, your existing photo and its verification remain unchanged.

6. Location Data

Approximate Location (Required)

  • What is collected: City-level or neighborhood-level location.
  • How it is collected: Derived from IP address or coarse device location permission.
  • Purpose: To match users with companions located in a similar area.
  • Precision: Not precise enough to identify a user's home address, workplace, or specific movements.

Precise GPS Location (Optional)

  • What is collected: GPS coordinates when the user is actively using location-based features.
  • How it is collected: Only when the user explicitly grants precise location permission through the device operating system prompt.
  • Purpose: To assist in identifying nearby public places suitable for meetups.
  • User control: This permission can be revoked at any time through the device's operating system settings. Revoking this permission will disable location-dependent features but will not affect other aspects of the service.

Meetup Location

  • When a booking is confirmed, the public place address selected by the user is shared with the matched companion for the purpose of facilitating the meetup.
  • This information is limited to the specific booking and is not made visible to other users of the platform.

What Humrah Does Not Do: Humrah does not track user location continuously or in the background. Humrah does not share precise GPS coordinates publicly or with other users. Humrah does not use location data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose not described in this policy. Users select their own meetup locations; Humrah does not direct users to specific locations.

7. Offline Meetings & Safety

Humrah is a communication and coordination platform. It enables users to identify potential companions and arrange to meet in person at public locations. Humrah does not organize, supervise, or participate in any offline meeting between users.

Platform Role and Limitations

The Humrah platform facilitates communication between users and assists in suggesting public places for meetups. Once a booking is confirmed, the actual meeting occurs independently and entirely outside the control of Humrah. Humrah does not verify the real-time identity, location, or conduct of any user during an offline meeting.

User Responsibility

Users are solely responsible for their personal safety when meeting another user offline. Users are advised to:

  • Meet only in well-lit, populated public locations.
  • Inform a trusted person of the meeting details before attending.
  • Trust their own judgment and leave any situation that feels unsafe.
  • Not share personal contact information (home address, personal phone number) with other users unless they choose to do so independently.

Safety Disclaimer

Humrah does not guarantee the behavior, character, or intentions of any user on the platform. The platform's verification mechanisms reduce the risk of fake or duplicate accounts but do not constitute a guarantee of safety. Humrah is not liable for any harm, loss, or injury arising from offline interactions between users.

Reporting and Moderation

Users may report concerns about another user's conduct directly within the application using the report function available on any user profile or booking page. Reports are reviewed by the Humrah moderation team. Where a reported user is found to have violated the Community Guidelines or Terms of Service, Humrah may suspend or permanently remove their account. Users may also contact the Grievance Officer at the details listed in Section 16 for urgent safety concerns.

9. Data Retention & Storage

Humrah retains personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. The following retention periods apply:

Active Account Data

  • Profile information (name, bio, photos, interests): Retained for the duration of the active account.
  • Face embeddings: Retained for the duration of the active account and for 30 days following account deletion.
  • In-app messages: Retained for 90 days from the date of the message, then permanently deleted.
  • Booking history: Retained for 12 months from the date of the booking.
  • Quick Connect call records: Deleted automatically 30 days after they are created. Quick Connect calls are not recorded.
  • Quick Connect invitation records: Deleted automatically 2 days after they are created, and when you delete your account. Your invitations setting and your acceptance of the Quick Connect terms are removed when you withdraw Quick Connect consent or delete your account. See Section 17.
  • Usage and log data: Retained for 180 days, then deleted.

Temporary and Verification Data

  • Verification video recordings: Deleted within 48 hours of processing. Not retained beyond this period under any circumstances.
  • Session data: Deleted upon logout or after 30 days of inactivity, whichever occurs first.
  • OTP and authentication tokens: Invalidated immediately after use or upon expiry.

Deleted Account Data

Upon account deletion, the following deletion schedule applies:

  • Immediate (within 24 hours): Account deactivated, profile removed from visibility on the platform.
  • Within 48 hours: Profile photos permanently deleted from storage.
  • Within 7 days: In-app messages permanently deleted.
  • Within 30 days: Face embeddings permanently deleted.
  • Within 90 days: Booking history, email address, and device identifiers permanently deleted.

Security Logs and Legal Hold Data

  • Security and fraud logs (IP address, device fingerprints): Retained for up to 180 days for fraud investigation and platform security purposes, then deleted.
  • Data subject to a legal hold or law enforcement request: Retained for the duration required by the applicable legal obligation, regardless of the standard retention periods above. Users will be informed of any such hold to the extent permitted by law.

Backups

Humrah maintains encrypted system backups for disaster recovery purposes. Backup data is overwritten or purged on a rolling cycle not exceeding 90 days. Deleted personal data will be removed from active systems within the timelines above and from backup systems within the applicable backup cycle.

No Contradictions: In all cases, the more specific retention period in this section governs. Where an account is deleted, backup copies of personal data are purged within the next scheduled backup cycle, not to exceed 90 days from the date of deletion.

17. Quick Connect Voice Calls

Quick Connect is an optional feature of the Humrah app for one-to-one voice calls with another eligible Humrah member, usually someone you do not already know. This section explains what Quick Connect processes and how.

How Matching Works

  • When you start searching, Humrah matches you with another eligible member based on interests from your profile answers. Matching does not use your location.
  • If two people have recently skipped each other, they are not matched again for a period of time.
  • A match is only a proposal. A call starts only if both of you accept it. After a call ends, Quick Connect does not start a new search by itself.
  • Quick Connect may be busy or temporarily unavailable. If it is busy, you can ask to be notified (see "Notify Me" below).

Smart Discovery Invitations

Smart Discovery helps a member who is searching find someone when no match has been found after a short while. It sends an invitation to join matchmaking. It never starts a call by itself.

  • Off unless you turn it on: You receive invitations only if Humrah has made Smart Discovery available and you have turned on "Invitations" on the Quick Connect screen. You can turn it off at any time.
  • Why you may be invited: When another member has been searching for a short while without a match, Humrah may invite a small number of members who have turned on invitations, are currently eligible for Quick Connect (see "Who Can Use Quick Connect"), are not already searching or in a call, have not blocked or been blocked by that member, have not recently skipped or reported each other, and share at least one interest with that member. Humrah limits how often the same person can be invited.
  • What an invitation shows: That someone who shares your interests would like a voice chat now, and which interests you share. It does not show the other member's name, photo or other details. The member who is searching is told only how many people were invited, not who.
  • Accepting: If you accept, Humrah checks again that you are both still eligible. If so, you are both shown an ordinary match, with first name, profile photo and shared interests, and a call starts only if you both accept that match. Accepting an invitation does not start a call or turn on your microphone.
  • Declining, expiry and cancellation: You can tap "Not now" in the app or on the notification. The other member is not told that you declined. An invitation can be accepted only for a short time. It is cancelled, and its notification removed where possible, if the other member stops searching, is matched with someone else or leaves Quick Connect, or if you turn invitations off. If either of you is no longer eligible when you accept, the invitation cannot be used.

Who Can Use Quick Connect

Quick Connect is available to members aged 18 or over, based on the date of birth and adult confirmation they provided, who have an approved photo verification (see Changing Your Profile Photo and Photo Verification), have accepted the current Quick Connect terms in the app, and whose account is in good standing. These checks are made by Humrah's servers.

What the Other Person Sees

  • Before any call starts, the other person sees your first name, your profile photo and up to five interests you share.
  • A call starts only if both of you accept the match.
  • Quick Connect does not show your email address, phone number or location to the other person.

Microphone and Call Audio

  • The app asks for microphone permission before your first search. Your microphone is used only during a call.
  • While a call continues with the app in the background, Android shows an ongoing notification.
  • Humrah does not record Quick Connect calls or store call audio.
  • Call audio is sent through a Humrah-operated relay server instead of directly between phones, so the other person does not receive your IP address. The relay server processes network information such as IP addresses only to deliver the audio. Messages needed to set up the call pass through Humrah's servers.
  • Calls last up to 30 minutes. Humrah may end a call if either account is restricted, if one person blocks the other, or if either person no longer meets the requirements in "Who Can Use Quick Connect", for example if a photo verification is no longer approved or Quick Connect consent is withdrawn.

What Humrah Keeps

  • Call records: who was matched, when, whether the match was accepted, how and when the call ended, and whether the match was based on shared interests. These records do not contain call audio and are deleted automatically 30 days after they are created.
  • Searches: a search is temporary and is removed when you stop searching, leave Quick Connect or after a short time.
  • Skipped matches: a short-lived record that two people skipped each other, so they are not matched again for a period of time.
  • Your acceptance of the Quick Connect terms: the version you accepted and when. It is kept with your account until you withdraw your Quick Connect consent or delete your account; either removes it.
  • Your invitations setting: whether you turned on Smart Discovery invitations and the notification token of the phone you turned it on from, so invitations can reach that phone. When you turn invitations off, the token is removed. Withdrawing your Quick Connect consent or deleting your account removes the setting.
  • Invitation records: who was invited for which search, the shared interests shown, whether the invitation was accepted, declined, expired or cancelled, how it was delivered, and when. These records are used to deliver invitations, to prevent repeated or duplicate invitations, to enforce invitation limits and to cancel invitations that are no longer valid. They are deleted automatically 2 days after they are created, and straight away if you delete your account.

Reports and Blocks

  • You can report the other person during a call or for up to 24 hours after it ends. Reports go to Humrah's safety team and are kept for as long as the safety team needs them.
  • You can also choose to block the other person. A blocked person is not matched with you again.

Notify Me

If Quick Connect is busy, you can ask to be notified. Humrah then keeps your request and your device's notification token for a limited time and may send you a push notification when a spot may be available. The notification only invites you to open Quick Connect; it does not start a search, a call or your microphone. You can cancel the request in the app.

How Invitation Data Is Used

To decide whether you can be invited, Humrah's servers compare the interests from your profile answers with those of the member who is searching, and check your Quick Connect eligibility and invitation limits. Invitations are delivered inside the app when you have Quick Connect open, or otherwise as a push notification through Firebase Cloud Messaging (a Google service) to the phone you turned invitations on from. The push notification contains the invitation's identifier, when it expires and a general message. It does not contain the other member's name or photo. Humrah uses this processing only to run Smart Discovery and keep it safe.

Withdrawing Quick Connect Consent

You can withdraw your Quick Connect consent at any time by tapping "Withdraw Quick Connect consent" on the Quick Connect screen. When you do:

  • Humrah deletes your records of accepting the Quick Connect terms, and you cannot use Quick Connect again until you accept them again.
  • Any search you started stops, a match being proposed is cancelled, and a call you are in ends straight away for both of you. The other person sees that the call has ended.
  • Your "Notify Me" request and your invitations setting are removed, and open invitations you sent or received are cancelled.
  • Reports already made, by you or about you, are kept for Humrah's safety team as described above. Call records are deleted on their usual schedule.

Withdrawing Quick Connect consent does not delete your Humrah account. When you delete your account, Humrah ends any Quick Connect activity and deletes your Quick Connect consent records, your invitations setting and every invitation you sent or received.

Your Choices

Quick Connect is optional. You can leave a search, skip a match or end a call at any time, turn Smart Discovery invitations on or off, and withdraw your Quick Connect consent as described above. To withdraw consent to Humrah's processing of your personal data in general, see Section 11.
